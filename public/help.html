Help and safety

How Fenn works, and how to stay safe while you rent or fill a room.

Staying safe

  • Never pay a deposit, a holding fee or first month's rent before you have seen the room in person or on a live video call.
  • Keep your conversation in Fenn. Your phone number and email stay private until you choose to share them.
  • Be careful with anyone who asks for payment by gift card, wire transfer or crypto, or who says they are out of the country.
  • Fenn shows the area of a room, not its address. The host shares the address once you are accepted.
  • If a listing or a message looks wrong, use Report. Our team reviews every report.

Looking for a room

  1. Watch room tours in Find. You can browse without an account.
  2. Make your Passport once: your move-in date, budget and a short hello.
  3. Tap Apply on a room. Your Passport is the application.
  4. Follow each application in your Inbox: Pending, Shortlisted, Chosen or Not chosen.

Filling your room

  1. Tap Fill and answer a few basics: rent, move-out date and what is included.
  2. Film three short shots standing still, or take photos. Fenn turns them into a tour.
  3. Share your link in a group chat or a housing group.
  4. Review applicants in Fenn, shortlist, and choose one. The others get a kind note.

What the badges mean

  • Phone verified: the person confirmed a phone number with a code.
  • ID verified: the person passed an ID check. Hosts and renters see the badge, never the document.
  • No badge means no check has been done yet. It does not mean anything is wrong.

Your listing and your data

  • You can edit a live listing from Fill at any time.
  • Deleting a listing removes its tour, its links, and its applications and messages for good.
  • Photos are stripped of location data before they are uploaded.
  • See the Privacy policy and the Terms.
